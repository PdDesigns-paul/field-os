What the spec still needs

Doctrine is solid. These are the holes that will hurt on a real phone or a Vercel bill.

01 Talk

Not a public URL

No account plus a public /api/talk is a credit firehose. Mint a shop grant from the desk. Still no login Place.

02 Adapter

Use adapter-vercel

adapter-static cannot be the Talk function. One SvelteKit app, prerendered Places, one server route.

03 Book

OPFS is not a disk

Safari will eat it. OPFS primary, IndexedDB snapshot as fallback. Schema version from day one.

04 Packs

Do not bundle all four

A pest phone must not ship i35 in the JS. Fetch the host’s pack. Leak tests on compiled surfaces and shipped bytes.

05 Day

Labor window, not midnight

Start opens a day. End closes it. Do not auto-roll a 9pm last door onto tomorrow’s card.

06 Voice

Typed fallback on iPhone

Hold-to-talk is not offline. Hear this line prefers a shop clip. Never record a homeowner.

The rest — photos stay in the camera roll, Plan is list-first, Office is a desk PIN, cooling-off is US federal only in V1 — lives in docs/GAPS.md.
